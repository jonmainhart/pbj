# PBJ Pool Rules

## Weekly Picks and Scoring

- Pick the winner of each NFL game.
- Correct pick = win `✅`
- Incorrect pick = loss `❌`
- NFL tie = tie `➖`
- N/P or missing pick = loss once the game is final `❌ N/P`
- Unfinished games do not count toward the weekly record.

Before a game is final:

- `⏳` Pending
- `⏳ N/P` Pending non-pick
- `🟢` Live — pick is winning or tied
- `🟡` Live — pick is losing

### Monday Night Tiebreaker

- Predict the combined score of the Monday night game or games.
- Closest prediction wins the tiebreaker.
- Multiple Monday games are combined into one total.
- Equal tiebreaker distances remain tied.

### Weekly Standings

Players are ranked by:

1. Most wins.
2. Closest Monday night tiebreaker.

Players tied in both share the same rank.

Competition ranking is used: `1, 2, 2, 4`.

Weekly standings become official after every game is final.

- `🏆` Rank 1 — weekly winner
- Tied Rank 1 players share the weekly win.
- Each tied winner receives one weekly win for season tracking.
- `💩` Players sharing the lowest rank each receive a last-place finish.

### Weekly Elimination

A player remains in contention if any possible combination of remaining game results allows that player to finish tied for the most wins.

A player is eliminated only when no such combination remains.

The Monday tiebreaker is not used for early elimination.

Players still in contention appear above the `ELIMINATED` divider.

Elimination does not affect scoring or final rankings.

## Season Standings

Players are ranked by:

1. Total wins.
2. Win %.

**Win %:** `(wins + 0.5 × ties) / (wins + losses + ties)`

NFL ties count as half a win when calculating Win %. N/P losses count as losses normally.

Players tied in both total wins and Win % share the same rank.

No additional statistic breaks a season tie.

Season indicators:

- `👑` Current first place
- `🏆` Number of weekly wins
- `💩` Number of last-place finishes

Weekly wins and last-place finishes are tracked but do not affect season ranking.
